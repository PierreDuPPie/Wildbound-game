Things to try

  1. Speak to Ranger Dan at Acacia Outpost and follow the objective.
  2. Explore Route 01. Weaken a wild creature in battle, then choose Wildbond.
  3. Follow the story into Riverine Woodland. Try a companion ability with G or the Abilities menu.
  4. Gather materials and build a Creature Shelter at the outpost using Camp.
  5. Care for your companions, investigate the mystery, and check Pack > Field Notes.
  6. Save, close the tab, and return in the same browser. Check your companions and buildings are still there.
Useful shortcuts

WASD move; Shift sprint; Space jump; E interact; C camp; Q pack; G abilities; X Atlas; Escape menu. With the equipment and a conscious companion, R rides and F requests flight or landing. On phones, play upright or sideways with the on-screen controls.

This is a single-player alpha. Phone performance and balance still need real player feedback. Saves stay on this browser and device.
