Wildbound

Something is stirring in the bushveld.
Form a Wildbond. Build a sanctuary. Follow Ratelit.

Computer: WASD move, Space jump, E interact, C camp, Q pack, G companion abilities. Escape opens the menu.
Phone: play upright or sideways. Steer with the left pad; use the right action buttons and Menu for camp, pack and touch settings.
