## 🧬 II. THE 100-CREATURE ECOLOGICAL BALANCE FORMULA

To ensure each continent feels like a living, breathing ecosystem rather than just a collection of big animals, every 100-creature continental roster strictly follows the **25 / 15 / 20 / 20 / 20 Ecological Ratio**:

```
                       TOTAL: 100 CREATURES PER CONTINENT
  ┌───────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 25% Micro-Fauna & Invertebrates   │ 15% Reptiles, Amphibians, River   │
  │ (Ants, Beetles, Spiders, Wasps)   │ (Snakes, Crocs, Frogs, Monitors)  │
  ├───────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 20% Birds & Aviary                │ 20% Small & Medium Mammals        │
  │ (Raptors, Songbirds, Flightless)  │ (Badgers, Foxes, Monkeys, Bats)   │
  └───────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
                      20% Megafauna, Apex & Giants
                      (Elephants, Lions, Rhinos, Zebras)
```

## 🎨 III. UNIFIED ART DIRECTION & PROMPT PIPELINE

To guarantee 100% visual harmony across all 600 creatures:

1. **Aesthetic Philosophy:**
   * **Grounded Stylized Wildlife:** Anatomy stays true to real biology (accurate leg joints, realistic eyes, natural silhouettes), but with clean hand-painted brush textures, warm lighting, and expressive companion personality.
   * **No Cartoon Disconnect:** Not chibi, not pixel art, and not hyper-realistic uncanny photorealism.
   * **Elemental Resonance:** Subtle elemental touches (e.g. slight amber starlight glow on Rollbug, solar highlights on Cheetan, crystalline earth crust on Warthusk) without turning them into fantasy monsters.

2. **Master Image Generation Prompt Standard:**
   ```
   [Subject]: A stylized companion portrait of [Animal Species / Wildbound Name], [action or pose],
   [Style]: Hand-illustrated indie wildlife adventure game concept art, rich natural textures, 
   clean expressive silhouette, grounded biological anatomy, warm natural rim lighting, 
   soft painterly brushstrokes, studio concept art on clean neutral background, 
   consistent with Wildbound aesthetic.
   ```

## 🎓 V. EDUCATIONAL FIELD GUIDE ARCHITECTURE

When a young player discovers an animal in the wild, the **Wild Atlas Entry** displays:
1. **Name & Real-World Inspiration:** Pronounceable name + authentic common and scientific names.
2. **Habitat & Ecosystem Role:** Explains where it lives and what it does for nature (recycler, predator, seed disperser).
3. **The "Superpower" Fact:** Fascinating, memorable biological traits (e.g. Dung Beetles navigating by the Milky Way; Vultures digesting anthrax; Elephants communicating through sub-audible seismic rumbles).
