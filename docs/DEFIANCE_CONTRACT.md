# Defiance vs Rulebreaker Doctrine

## Defiance Contract
**Aspect: Defiance** (Found on Ratelit)
- **Condition**: Triggers when Vitality drops below 30% of max.
- **Effect**: Grants a +25% bonus to Power.
- **Intent**: Represents a burst of adrenaline and stubbornness when cornered.

## Rulebreaker Doctrine
Any mechanics or moves that bypass standard turn order or damage calculation must explicitly declare themselves as "Rulebreakers". Currently, only `Sentry` (Priority +1) is a minor Rulebreaker. Defiance alters stats, not rules, and strictly adheres to standard calculation phases.
